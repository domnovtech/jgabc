PSALM 70

1  *For the Choirmaster. Of David. A Memorial.*

2  O God, come to my assistance;
   O LORD, make haste to help me!
3  Let there be shame and confusion
   on those who seek my life.

   O let them be turned back in confusion,
   who delight in my harm;
4  let them turn because of their shame,
   who jeer at me and mock.

5  O let there be rejoicing and gladness
   for all who seek you.
   Let them say forever, “God is great,”
   who love your saving help.

6  As for me, who am wretched and poor,
   hasten to me, O God.
   You are my rescuer, my help;
   O LORD, do not delay.
